Simple Omelette Recepie

An easy and quick dish,perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs
cooked to perfection, optionally filled with ur choice of cheese, vegitables, or meat

Preparation time
  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Perparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes
  • Please help me!!!

Ingredients

  • 3-4 large eggs
  • salt, to taste
  • pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • optional filling: cheese, diced vegitables, cooked meats, herb

Instruction

  1. Beat the eggs:In a bowl, beat the egg with a pinch of salt and pepper until they are well
    You can add a table walter or milk for a fulffer texture.
  2. Heat the pan:place a non-stick frying pan over a medium heat and add butter and oil.
  3. Cook the omolette:Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to ensure
    to ensure the eggs evenly coat the surface.
  4. Add fillings (Optional):When the eggs begins to set at the edge but are still slightly
    runny in the middle,sprinkle your chosen filling over one half of the omelette.
  5. Fold and serve:As the omelette continues to cook, carefully lift one edge and fold it over
    the filling, let it cook for another, minute, then slide it onto a plate
  6. Enjoy:Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if needed
